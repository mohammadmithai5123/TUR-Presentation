ZELLO / EVENT COMMUNICATION

Zello – Event Communication

Example Zello screen supplied by the organiser

OVERVIEW

Walkie-Talkie on Your Mobile

Use Zello for real-time communication throughout the event.

Coordinate with teams across checkpoints and event locations.

Available for download on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

GROUPS & CHANNELS

Dedicated Channels for Each Team

Create separate channels for different event teams.

Assign members to their respective channels.

Use designated channels for team coordination and updates.

PUSH-TO-TALK

Press, speak and release

Open the assigned event channel.

Hold the talk button and speak your update.

Release the button to listen to the team.

INTERNET CONNECTION

Mobile data stays on

Use an active 3G / 4G data connection.

Zello also works through connected Wi-Fi.

Internet is required for voice communication.
